Combines "Brand" + a short word, checks if the .com is open, shows only the ones under your price cap.

Ready.

How it works: availability is checked live against the official .com registry (Verisign RDAP). An unregistered .com is a standard-price domain (~$10-15 at most registrars), so it passes the cap. A registered domain is skipped. Premium-priced domains (rare for compound names like these) can't be detected without a registrar API key, so each hit has quick links to confirm the exact price before you buy.
