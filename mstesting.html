Before you call

Questions people ask

How much does lawn mowing cost in Algona?

It depends on the size of the yard and how often it gets cut. Matthew looks at the property, gives you a price, and the estimate is free — no charge for him to come look.

Do you mow every week, or can I book a one-time cut?

Both. Most customers are on a weekly or every-other-week route through the season, but one-time cuts are fine, including yards that have gotten away from you.

What towns do you serve besides Algona?

Algona is home base, and Matthew regularly works the nearby Kossuth County towns — Burt, Whittemore, Sexton, Irvington, Wesley, Titonka, Bancroft, Lu Verne and Lone Rock. Farther out, ask.

Do you do snow removal in the winter?

Yes. Driveways and walks get cleared after the snow stops. Same number year round.

What’s the fastest way to reach Matthew?

Text 515-341-6365. He’s out running equipment most of the day, so a text usually gets answered quicker than a voicemail. Email works for anything longer: contact@mslawncare.org.
